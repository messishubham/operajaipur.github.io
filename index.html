Our Story

A little Italy,
set inside Jaipur.

Opera was imagined as a warm Italian home in Jaipur — built around fire, flour, hand-rolled pastas, blistered Neapolitan pizzas, and candlelit conversations. Not a destination. A place you come back to.

From slow lunches to late evening tables, every detail is designed to feel personal: the warmth of the room, the rhythm of the kitchen, and the comfort of food made entirely by hand.

The Craft

What We Serve

From Neapolitan Pizzas
to Hand-Rolled Pastas

Opera's menu moves from blistered wood-fired pizzas to hand-rolled pastas, small plates made for sharing, and fresh cocktails for slow evenings — built for everyday Italian comfort in Jaipur.

Wood-fired · Neapolitan

Blistered in our wood-fired oven, built on San Marzano tomatoes and fior di latte — crisp at the edge, pillowy at the centre. Exactly as Napoli intended.

Wood-fired Spicy Margherita

San Marzano, fior di latte, fresh basil, Calabrian chilli, EVOO.

Wood-fired Death by Meat

Slow-roasted pepperoni, Italian sausage, smoked mozzarella, chilli flakes.

Wood-fired Hot Bell Pepper

Roasted mixed peppers, jalapeño, fresh mozzarella, tomato base.

Wood-fired Quattro Formaggi

Mozzarella, Gorgonzola, Parmigiano, smoked scamorza, honey drizzle.

Wood-fired Truffle Funghi

White truffle oil, wild mushrooms, fontina, fresh thyme, crème fraîche.

Hand-rolled · Daily

Every pasta starts from scratch — rolled by hand, shaped to order, finished with slow sauces. This is Italian comfort in its most honest form.

Hand-rolled Mushroom Ravioli

Wild mushroom & ricotta filling, brown butter, fresh sage, Parmigiano.

Hand-rolled Pesto Pasta

Trofie, house-pressed Genovese basil pesto, pine nuts, Parmigiano.

Hand-rolled Fettuccine Barbabietola

Silky beetroot cream, goat cheese crumble, toasted walnuts, fresh dill.

Slow sauce Aglio Olio

Spaghetti, slow-toasted garlic, Calabrian chilli, EVOO, flat-leaf parsley.

Roman classic Cacio e Pepe

Tonnarelli, Pecorino Romano, coarse black pepper — three ingredients, no shortcuts.

For the table · Antipasti

Light, generous, and made for sharing. Our small plates open the meal and invite you to slow down — best ordered alongside a glass of something Italian.

For the table Arancini

Crispy saffron risotto balls, truffle aioli, aged Parmigiano.

House-made Burrata Sourdough

House burrata, charred sourdough, San Marzano confit, basil oil.

For the table Guacamole Chip & Dip

Smashed avocado, lime, chilli flakes, house-fried tortilla chips.

House-made Mushroom Bruschetta

Wild mushroom ragù, whipped ricotta, toasted ciabatta, thyme.

Light & crisp Zucchini Fritti

Beer-battered zucchini ribbons, lemon-herb dipping sauce.

Sweet finish · Italian

No hurry. Desserts at OPERA are made for those who linger — espresso-soaked, warm from the oven, or simply cold and silky.

Italian classic Tiramisu

Espresso-soaked savoiardi, mascarpone cream, dark cocoa — the original.

Warm from the oven Skillet Cookie

Dark chocolate chip cookie, baked to order, vanilla gelato on top.

House-made Pistachio Tres Leches

Pistachio sponge, three-milk soak, whipped cream, crushed pistachios.

Silky & cold Panna Cotta

Vanilla panna cotta, seasonal berry compote, candied orange zest.

Warm from the oven Chocolate Lava

Molten Valrhona dark chocolate cake, Chantilly cream, cocoa dust.

Evening pours · Fresh cocktails

Crafted for candlelit tables and slow conversations. Italian aperitivos, fresh-shaken cocktails, and long pours — made to match the pace of the evening.

Evening pour Picante

Jalapeño-infused tequila, fresh lime, agave, cucumber, smoked salt rim.

Italian aperitivo Aperol Spritz

Aperol, Prosecco, soda, fresh orange — the Italian aperitivo, done properly.

Stirred, not shaken Negroni

Gin, Campari, sweet vermouth, orange peel — a Roman evening in a glass.

Light & floral Hugo Spritz

Elderflower, Prosecco, fresh mint, lime, soda — easy and aromatic.

House limoncello Limoncello Sour
